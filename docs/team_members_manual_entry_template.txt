UOC Football - Team Members Manual Entry Template

Purpose:
Use this file to prepare all data before adding a team manually in Team Management.
You can copy/paste values quickly while creating a team.

Validation Rules:
- Season: required
- Team status: present or past
- Player NIC: exactly 12 digits
- Coach NIC: exactly 12 digits
- Player phone number: exactly 10 digits
- Coach phone number: exactly 10 digits
- Only 1 Captain per team
- Only 1 Vice-Captain per team
- Default account password after creation: 123456
- Username for player/coach login: NIC
